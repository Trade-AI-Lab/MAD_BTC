### **Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)**

**Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)** is an investment strategy in which an investor divides their total investment amount into periodic purchases of a target asset, regardless of its price. This method reduces the impact of market volatility by spreading the investment over time.

---

### **How DCA Works**
1. **Fixed Investment Amount**: The investor commits to investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (e.g., weekly, monthly).
2. **Buy at Different Prices**: Because the asset price fluctuates, the fixed amount buys more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high.
3. **Average Cost**: Over time, the investor achieves an average purchase price for the asset, mitigating the risk of investing a lump sum at an unfavorable price.

---

### **Benefits of DCA**
1. **Reduces Emotional Decision-Making**: Investors avoid the temptation to time the market or make impulsive decisions based on fear or greed.
2. **Mitigates Risk**: By investing in smaller amounts over time, DCA reduces the risk of committing all capital during market highs.
3. **Simplicity**: It is easy to implement and does not require constant monitoring of the market.
